Zhuo Chen

King's College London, London, UK.

ICRA2024, Yokohama, Japan

Hi! I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the Robot Perception Lab (RPL) at King’s College London, supervised by Prof. Shan Luo. I am also a Research Scientist Intern at the NVIDIA Seattle Robotics Lab, hosted by Yashraj Narang and Iretiayo Akinola. In addition, I collaborate closely with Prof. Jiankang Deng at Imperial College London on embodied AI.

I am passionate about multimodal Physical AI, with the goal of enabling robots to perceive, reason, and act robustly in the physical world through vision and touch. My research focuses on sensor-invariant physical representations, generative modeling, and cross-embodiment learning for contact-rich robot manipulation. In particular, my Ph.D. research advances robotic dexterity through human-level tactile intelligence along three interconnected directions: (1) force and tactile sensing for precise robot grapsing and manipulation; (2) foundation models that unify heterogeneous tactile sensors, from vision-based tactile sensors to electronic skins; and (3) vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) models for contact-rich robot manipulation. My work spans the full robotics stack—from sensor fabrication to physical simulation, sim-to-real transfer, and learning-based methods including generative learning, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning.

Beyond research, I actively contribute to the robotics community as a reviewer for T-RO, RA-L, RSS, ICRA, and IROS. I was selected as a member of the 2026 cohort of RSS Pioneers. I also serve as Program Chair of the 7th ViTac Workshop at ICRA and previously served as Local Arrangements Chair of the 6th UK Robot Manipulation Workshop.
