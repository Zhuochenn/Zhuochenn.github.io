Hi! I am a `final-year Ph.D. candidate` in the [Robot Perception Lab (RPL)](https://kings-rpl.github.io/) at King's College London, supervised by Prof. [Shan Luo](https://shanluo.github.io/). I am also a Research Scientist Intern at the [NVIDIA Seattle Robotics Lab](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/srl/), hosted by [Yashraj Narang](https://research.nvidia.com/person/yashraj-narang) and [Iretiayo Akinola](https://research.nvidia.com/person/iretiayo-akinola). In addition, I collaborate closely with Prof. [Jiankang Deng](https://jiankangdeng.github.io/) at Imperial College London on embodied AI.

I am passionate about `multimodal Physical AI`, with the goal of enabling robots to perceive, reason, and act robustly in the physical world through `vision and touch`. My research focuses on sensor-invariant physical representations, generative modeling, and cross-embodiment learning for contact-rich robot manipulation. In particular, my Ph.D. research advances robotic dexterity through human-level `tactile intelligence` along three interconnected directions: (1) force and tactile sensing for precise robot grapsing; (2) foundation models that unify heterogeneous tactile sensors, from vision-based tactile sensors to electronic skins; and (3) vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) models for contact-rich robot grasping and manipulation. My work spans the full robotics stack—from sensor fabrication to physical simulation, sim-to-real transfer, and learning-based methods including generative learning, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning.

Beyond research, I actively contribute to the robotics community as a reviewer for T-RO, RA-L, RSS, ICRA, and IROS. I was selected as a member of the 2026 cohort of [RSS Pioneers](https://sites.google.com/view/rsspioneers2026/participants?authuser=0). I also serve as Program Chair of the 7th [ViTac Workshop](https://shanluo.github.io/ViTacWorkshops/vitac2026) at ICRA and previously served as Local Arrangements Chair of the [6th UK Robot Manipulation Workshop](https://www.robot-manipulation.uk/).
